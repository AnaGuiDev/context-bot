## How to run the real migration

1. Pick one of your bots on yellow or red (ask it "what colour are we?").
2. Open Context Bot Migrator (https://x.ai/bot/oUJEAr2axANgtedIGrkNS) and tell it which bot to migrate.
3. Paste the stage 1 line it gives you into the old bot and copy the counts back.
4. Review `TRASPASO.md` before going on; remove anything you don't want to carry over.
5. Say yes to create the new bot and let it pass the checklist with routines paused.
6. Use the new bot for a couple of days and note your impressions.
7. Send us: inventory counts, handoff length, whether the checklist passed, what was missing and your impression. No private data.
